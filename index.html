Sources: names from Wikipedia; dates, gender, IMDb IDs and screen credits from Wikidata; photographs from Wikimedia Commons (click a photo in the table for its licence and photographer). The photo is the image Wikipedia uses for the actor's article, or failing that the most recent dated photo in their Commons category. Unreleased films are left out. Films are ranked by how many language editions of Wikipedia cover them, which tracks how widely known a film is.

Last appearance is the most recent dated screen credit Wikidata lists for the actor: a film or episode by its original release, or a TV series by when the actor's role ended (or, failing that, when the series ended, ignoring series that ran on after the actor's death). Long-running series still on air with no recorded end are left out, and Wikidata's credits are incomplete for many TV and stage actors.

When this page is hosted next to a saved copy (a data folder and a photos folder made by the sync script), it reads names, dates, credits and photos from there and asks Wikipedia only for names added since the copy was made. Opened on its own, it looks up only the selected letter, loads photos one actor at a time in the table's order, and keeps photos in this browser for 30 days and names, dates and credits for 7 days.
